## Source and scope

[NHS England RTT Waiting Times](https://www.england.nhs.uk/statistics/statistical-work-areas/rtt-waiting-times/) publishes the monthly extracts. The scripts use exact `Part_2` incomplete pathways, exclude `NONC`, and sum the weekly bands. The February 2019 baseline has the historical 52+ schema documented in the manifest.

## Retrieval and reproducibility

The canonical analysis scripts are copied into `analysis/`. Run `python analysis/make_figures.py` from this pack after installing Python 3.10+, DuckDB, pandas, and matplotlib. Outputs are written to `outputs/` and figures to `figures/.`
